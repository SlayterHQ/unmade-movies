Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Unmade Movies collects, uses, and protects your information. Unmade Movies is operated by SLAYTER.

2. Information Collected

We collect information you provide directly (name, email, submissions, comments) and information collected automatically (IP address, browser type, device info, site usage).

3. Public Content Warning

Unmade Movies is a public community. Ideas, prompts, comments, and usernames are publicly visible. Do not submit private or confidential information.

4. How We Use Information

We use your data to operate the site, manage accounts, process submissions, and send updates where permitted.

5. Sharing and Retention

We share data with service providers (hosting, database, analytics) as needed. We retain information as long as necessary for operation and compliance.
