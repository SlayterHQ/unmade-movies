Community Rules

Last updated: May 25, 2026

1. Purpose

Unmade Movies exists to help people share movie-worthy ideas, prompts, and conversations in public. Our goal is to make the board interesting, generous, safe, and creatively useful.

2. Core Principles

  • Public-minded.
  • Respectful.
  • Creative.
  • Honest.
  • Constructive.

3. What to Post

We welcome original movie ideas, prompt questions that invite strong answers, loglines, and constructive comments that help make an idea stronger.

4. What Not to Post

Do not post confidential material, harassment, hate speech, spam, or anything you do not have the right to share. This is a public board; keep it safe and professional.

5. Respect Creative Boundaries

Users are free to discuss ideas, prompts, and hooks. Do not pretend to own someone else's work or misrepresent industry interest. Comments should aim to sharpen the premise and suggest stronger hooks.
